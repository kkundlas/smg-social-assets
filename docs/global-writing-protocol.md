# Global Writing Protocol (all bots, all writing)

Mandatory rule set by Kulmeet Kundlas MD on October 2, 2026. Not optional. No content ships without it.

## Who and what it covers

Every bot and assistant that writes anything for Shield Medical Group or Kulmeet Kundlas MD. That includes:

- Facebook posts, Reels, Stories, group shares, and the Tuesday news item
- Every other social channel (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, X, Pinterest, Google Business Profile)
- Captions, titles, descriptions, scripts, and on-screen text
- Emails, texts, DMs, and any other messages drafted for Kulmeet or the practice
- Any other text a bot generates, including handouts, briefs, and reports meant to be read by people

## The rules

1. **No AI tags or labels.** Never add AI-generated tags, labels, hashtags, watermarks, or tool names. No "AI-generated," "made with AI," "#AI," "generated by," or similar in the text or the artwork.
2. **Normal punctuation only.** No em dashes and no other AI-style punctuation. Use periods, commas, colons, and parentheses. No en dashes for ranges (write "1 to 2" or "Monday to Friday"), no decorative symbols, arrows, middle dots, checkmark lists, or emoji walls.
3. **Humanizer pass on everything.** Run every piece of written content through the humanizer (skill: LGJ humanizer, `lgj-humanizer`) before it is sent, scheduled, or published. It should sound like a real person talking: plain words, contractions, varied sentence length, no stock filler.
4. **No exceptions.** Nothing goes out, to Buffer or anywhere else, until it passes rules 1 to 3.

The one thing that comes before rule 1 is a disclosure the law or the platform requires (for example, Meta's AI disclosure for a realistic cloned voice or photorealistic synthetic video). In that case the bot does not post. It stops and asks Kulmeet first.

## Final check before anything ships

- [ ] Search the text for em dash and en dash characters. There should be none.
- [ ] No AI tags, labels, hashtags, watermarks, or tool names
- [ ] Humanizer pass done, and the text reads naturally out loud
- [ ] Channel rules still apply on top of this. For Shield Facebook that means the "Dr. Kundlas" byline, Shield brand only, and the disclaimer in the caption. See `docs/shield-facebook-posting-protocol.md`.

## Already scheduled or drafted work

This applies to anything already scheduled or sitting in drafts. Fix it before it goes out, and keep the media and the scheduled time.
