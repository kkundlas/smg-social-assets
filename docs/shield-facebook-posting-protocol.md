# Shield Medical Group Facebook Posting Protocol

Standing rule set by Kulmeet Kundlas MD on October 2, 2026.

Applies to every Shield Medical Group Facebook page post, Sebring first and Lake Wales second. That includes Feed posts, Reels, Stories, group shares, and the Tuesday news item. It applies to all future work and to anything already scheduled in Buffer. Scheduled posts get re-checked and edited before they go out.

## The checks (all happen before anything goes to Buffer)

1. **No AI tags or labels.** Never add AI-generated tags, labels, or hashtags to a post or caption. That means no "AI-generated," "made with AI," "#AI," or tool watermarks or tool names in the text or the artwork.
   - One exception: if Meta's own policy requires its AI disclosure (for example, photorealistic video or a realistic cloned voice of a real person), follow the platform rule and flag it to Kulmeet before scheduling.
2. **Normal punctuation only.** No em dashes or other AI-style punctuation in captions. Use periods, commas, colons, and parentheses. Skip curly or "smart" quote clutter, stacked ellipses, decorative symbols, arrows, and emoji walls.
3. **Humanizer pass.** Run every caption and all post text through the humanizer (skill: LGJ humanizer, `lgj-humanizer`) before publishing. It should read like a person talking: plain words, contractions, short sentences, no filler phrases like "In today's world" or "It's important to note."
4. **Timing of the checks.** Checks 1 to 3 and check 5 happen before the post is created or edited in Buffer. Nothing is scheduled until the caption passes all of them.
5. **Brand, byline, and disclaimer.**
   - Byline: Dr. Kundlas, on its own line, with no dash in front.
   - Shield Medical Group brand only on Facebook. No Kundlas MD logo, colors, or handles.
   - The educational disclaimer goes in the caption, never in the artwork:
     "For general education only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911."

## Pre-Buffer checklist

- [ ] No AI tags, labels, hashtags, or tool watermarks
- [ ] No em dashes or AI-style punctuation (search the text for the em dash and en dash characters)
- [ ] Humanizer pass done and the caption reads naturally out loud
- [ ] Byline reads "Dr. Kundlas"
- [ ] Shield Medical Group branding only
- [ ] Disclaimer is in the caption, not the artwork
- [ ] Sebring scheduled before Lake Wales
- [ ] Kulmeet approved the post (nothing auto-publishes)
- [ ] Group shares also pass the FB Group Compliance Guard (one post per group per 7 days, max 15 group shares per day, no promo-ban groups)

## Already scheduled posts

Any post already sitting in Buffer gets the same checks. If it fails, fix the text in Buffer (keeping the image and the scheduled time) before it goes live. Posts that are already live are only edited on Facebook if Kulmeet asks.
