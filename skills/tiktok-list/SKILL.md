# TikTok list

Use when Kulmeet says **TikTok list**, **/tiktok-list**, or wants a one-take talking-head TikTok whose left-side numbered list fills in as he talks. Apply [Shield Medical brand](sand-workflow:shield-medical-brand) as accents only. This is not [TikTok reel](sand-workflow:tiktok-reel) and not [TikTok explainer](sand-workflow:tiktok-explainer). Independent of Facebook. Never auto-publish.

Technique lock (copy the edit, not the person, topic, or claims): Dallin Holt Reel `https://www.facebook.com/reel/4510269485912634`. Local ref if present: `/workspace/fb-style-ref/source.mp4`.

## Locked shape

- Canvas **1080×1920**, about **30–45 seconds**.
- **One unbroken take.** Medium close-up, chest-up, eye level, looking into the lens.
- Zero cuts. Zero zooms. Zero B-roll. Zero logo sting. Zero intro card.

## Locked graphics (on the whole time)

1. **Top center title.** White rounded pill, bold black sans, one-line hook. Under it a smaller black pill: `(in N steps)`. Keep both out of TikTok’s top UI. No “intro” or slide numbers.
2. **Left-side list.** Numbers `1.` through `N.` visible from frame 1. When that step is spoken, the line first types the **equation**, then snaps to the **result**. Do not dump the full list at the start.
3. **Bottom karaoke captions.** Bold sans, 2–5 words, white with black outline. Live word in Signal Yellow `#F5D10A`. Keep above TikTok’s caption/comment bar.
4. **Last 2 seconds.** Swap the caption for `Save for later` with `later` in yellow. Tiny end mark only if he asks.

## Locked audio

On-camera voice only. Original footage only. Never clone face or voice. No music. No SFX on the text pops.

## Pacing

Jump straight into `Number one…` No welcome. One step every ~6–10 seconds. N defaults to 5 (range 3–7).

## Voice and caption

- Patient-facing: Dr. Kundlas. Honest. No miracle claims.
- Topic: `How [everyday health thing] actually works (in N steps)` with real sourced numbers.
- Buffer caption: one-line hook, the N steps in prose, `— Dr. Kundlas`, 3–5 hashtags, `Educational only — please talk to your clinician about you.` No Facebook Page tags.

## Workflow

1. Topic + hook line + N. Wait.
2. Spoken script and list (equation → result per line). Wait.
3. Shoot or use original A/V. Overlay the locked graphics. Show the cut. Wait. Do not post.
4. After yes: [Shield TikTok publish](sand-workflow:shield-tiktok-publish) as video, `customScheduled`. Never `shareNow` unless he says post now.

## Never

Do not render before script approval. Do not add music. Do not cut the take. Do not copy the reference creator’s face or claims. Do not post to Facebook from this skill.
