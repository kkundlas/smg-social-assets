Use when Kulmeet says **create Sketch reel**, **Sketch reel**, or wants a short vertical sketch-style educational reel. Not Daily reel. Not a chalkboard short. Not a talking-head.

# Sketch reel

Two flavors (same brand rules):

1. **Metaphor (~10–12s)** — paper-sketch or stick-schematic struggle→break (labeled ropes/stairs). One action snap.
2. **Guideline / teaching (~45–55s readable)** — teal-field chalk or stick-schematic multi-card (GLP-1 density). Dense labeled clusters, crimson leaders, little dead space. Prefer Higgsfield chalk or Hyperframes/HTML sketch; show both when he asks to compare.

## How he invokes it

`create Sketch reel` plus one sentence, or Media Manager hands a guideline topic. If no topic, ask for one sentence.

## Locked craft (Sep 5, 2026)

- Canvas **1080×1920**. Shield logo for Facebook; Kundlas MD for TikTok unless he names one cut.
- Colors: Deep Teal `#12343B`, Signal Yellow `#F5D10A`, Crimson `#96151D`, Soft Black `#231F20`, white. Bebas Neue + Lato.
- **Density:** GLP-1-style — mid-frame filled with labeled schematic cluster (3–6 chips + leaders). Empty/shallow mid-frame is a fail.
- **Readable pacing (locked):** hold **~9.5–10s per card**, fade **0.4s**. Do **not** use 5.5–7s holds for teaching cards — he said fast cuts are not readable. Metaphor stills may stay ~5s each.
- **SAFE_BOTTOM 300px:** yellow source chip at top of zone; empty teal below; no body text in the chrome zone; no text/chip overlap.
- Music: Rhythmic Reverie (~0.28–0.36). No ripped Facebook audio. No face/voice clone. No viral artwork copy.
- Educational only. Verify clinical ages/intervals against primary sources (e.g. AUA/SUO), not blogs.

## Guideline card order (when teaching PSA / similar)

1. Title — shared decision-making comes first  
2. **First Screening Test** — e.g. “PSA is the first test for prostate cancer screening”  
3. When to Start (ages / risk)  
4. Screening Cadence  
5. Ask at next visit + personalize + contact (one educational line)

Show clear on-screen messages per card in chat when he asks. Caption byline **Kulmeet Kundlas MD**.

## Workflow

1. Write card messages (or metaphor). Confirm if unclear.
2. Build dense cut. Prefer readable holds from the start.
3. Show MP4 (+ stills if useful) in Daily Reel chat. Wait for explicit yes.
4. After yes: GitHub `kkundlas/smg-social-assets` then Buffer at times he names (Shield Facebook, Kundlas MD TikTok). Never native-post.

## Never

Do not clone viral drawings, his face, or his voice. Do not use this slot for the five-news Daily reel. Do not post without yes. Do not ship an empty/shallow schematic or a too-fast teaching cut.
