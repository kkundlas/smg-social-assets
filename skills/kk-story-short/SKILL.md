# KK Story Short — Hook, last line, foreshadow

## Overview
Write one **YouTube Short** for **Kundlas MD (@kundlasmd)** using a story spine, not exam-room chapter headings.

This skill is the missing piece from the viral Shorts process Kulmeet supplied: write the **hook**, then the **last line**, then the **foreshadow**, then the middle. First and last are remembered most. The middle moves with **but / therefore**, not and-then.

Speak as one doctor in the exam room. Lived experience is clinic pattern only. No PHI. No invented patient.

Script + title pack only. No frames, MP4, or publish.

## Required: Hook skill first
Before any spoken script, run Drive **09-hook** (kundlas-md-hook).

- Need: title (approved or Recommended from the title pack), category, primary keyword, core misconception, payoff.
- Emit three hook candidates (Counter-Narrative, Empathy + Authority Bridge, High-Stakes Mistake), each with Spoken Lines and Editor Instructions.
- Mark one **Recommended**.
- The Recommended Spoken Lines **are** the Hook. Drop them in **verbatim**. Do not write a homemade hook.
- If Kulmeet already locked a hook in this chat, use that verbatim and skip a new pack.
- If title, misconception, or payoff is missing, emit `[HOOK INPUT INCOMPLETE — missing: X]` and stop. Do not write the script.

## When to use (vs kk1 / kk2)
- Use **kk-story-short** when Kulmeet wants her method, a story open, or hook-then-last-line order.
- Use [kk1-short](sand-workflow:kk1-short) when the topic needs five-heading calm closure and a shouldn’t/won’t bridge.
- Use [kk2-short](sand-workflow:kk2-short) when the topic is a 30–45s emotional PEACE punch.
- If Kulmeet names this skill, use it. Do not fall back to kk1 headings.

## Defaults
- Audience: adults 50+ / Medicare
- Platform: YouTube Shorts (slower, more story than TikTok; denser than a long-form open)
- Runtime: about 45–75 seconds
- Clinical focus: education / screening / prevention
- No spoken CTA, subscribe, or subhook

## Idea check (only if the topic is not already locked)
Pool, then pick one. Do not invent a new medical claim to make a better hook.
1. Watch others for the frame they used, not for facts to copy.
2. What video would Kulmeet want to watch on this topic?
3. AI may suggest angles. It may not invent stats, DOIs, or symptoms.
4. Lived experience: a sentence he actually says in clinic. No named patient. No chart.

Then choose **one** idea and run the write order below.

## Write order (do not skip or reorder)
Do this on paper before the spoken script. Show the four lines to Kulmeet, then the spoken script.

1. **Hook** — run 09-hook. The Recommended Spoken Lines are the hook, verbatim.
2. **Last line** — the one sentence they should remember. Write it now, before the middle.
3. **Foreshadow** — one line that sets the expectation: what they will get if they stay.
4. **Transition** — one punchy line into the meat.
5. **Rough script** — hook, foreshadow, transition, two or three but/therefore beats, last line.
6. Stop. Kulmeet films a rough.
7. Revisit, tighten, finalize. Do not treat the first pass as locked unless he says it is.

## Script spine (do not rename)
Return the spoken script under these labels, in this order:

```text
Hook
Foreshadow
Transition
But / therefore
Last line
```

### Hook
- First words on camera.
- The 09-hook Recommended Spoken Lines, verbatim.
- Do not open with “in this video.”
- Do not replace the hook pack with a homemade line.

### Foreshadow
- Tells the viewer what they will get if they stay.
- Sets the expectation. That is the job.
- Example shape: Stay with me. I will give you the one number that proves X.

### Transition
- Bridges the open into the teaching.
- Short. One line.

### But / therefore
- Two or three turns. Not a list.
- Each turn is conflict, then consequence: **but** (the feeling is wrong / the list is wrong / the age does not match), **therefore / so** (here is what to do with that).
- Lots of change. Rising action. No and-then fact stack.
- One core idea only.

### Last line
- Same job as the first line: they remember this.
- Calm, final, office-visit close.
- Must match the line you wrote in step 2, or a tighter revision of it.

## Title pack (mandatory)
Use Kulmeet’s locked formulas. Exactly 5 options. Each **≤50 characters** with the count. All three formulas represented. Mark one Recommended.

1. Action/Benefit: How to [Achieve Desire] Without [Pain Point]
2. Curiosity Gap: Why [Common Practice] is Actually [Unexpected Result]
3. Listicle: [Number] Warning Signs of [Problem] You Shouldn't Ignore

If a title is already locked, put it in the meta line and still offer 4 alternates only if useful.

## Output format
1. Hook pack (3 options + Recommended) unless a hook is already locked
2. The four build lines (Hook / Last line / Foreshadow / Transition)
3. Title pack
4. One-line meta (not spoken): `KK-STORY · Topic · Title: "<recommended or chosen>" · ~Ns`
5. Spoken script under Hook / Foreshadow / Transition / But / therefore / Last line
6. **One complete sentence per line**
7. Blank line between sections
8. No emojis, hashtags, or spoken CTA

## Style
- Warm, steady, plain English
- YouTube Shorts pace: a little story, not TikTok density, not long-form lecture
- Define jargon in the next breath
- Kundlas MD voice, not Shield Medical Group
- No miracle claims, no fear-bait, no “banned / illegally / they don’t want you to know” unless the evidence is real and the brand would say it

## Clinical safety
- Never invent statistics, study names, cutoffs, or coverage rules
- Never invent prediabetes symptoms (CDC / ADA / NIDDK: usually no clear symptoms)
- Never diagnose a viewer
- Prefer screening / reason to talk with a clinician
- No PHI, no named patient, no chart detail
- No berberine, snack-list cures, or “reverse in 30 days”
- Educational only
