# Kundlas MD Speaker Clear

Hard rail for every Kundlas MD long-form and short. Locked 2026-08-24 from Prediabetes Diet Mistakes review, then extended the same day from Kulmeet’s notepad speaker-on-Napkin sketch.

## Rule

**Nothing overlaps.** A box must never cover the speaker. A speaker PIP must never cover Napkin text, icons, or a teaching claim. Shrink, move, or reflow. Never grow a teal field to fill half the frame.

Exception: the last scene is a full-frame DISCLAIMER card with no speaker. That card is required and does not violate this rail.

## Anatomy that stays

Dark background. Three colors on it: yellow kicker, white claim, red underline (MYTH 5 / MISTAKE 2). Shape and placement can change. That anatomy does not. Red underline sits under the word ink, never through the letters.

## Last-scene disclaimer (hardwired)

Every video ends on the locked card: heading DISCLAIMER, crimson rule, the three educational-not-advice sentences. Exact copy. Hold 8s+.

## Napkin seats (four, pick by format and empty space)

Napkin is the full-frame background. Speaker is a small PIP. Crop-to-fill the PIP. Never stretch.

1. Portrait: speaker window high
2. Portrait: small video rectangle, bottom-left
3. Landscape 16:9: speaker rectangle, bottom-left
4. Landscape 16:9: speaker circle, bottom-left

YouTube long-form uses 3 or 4. Reels/Shorts use 1 or 2. Choose the seat that sits in empty Napkin space. If it would hit type or icons, move the PIP first; if it still hits, reflow the Napkin.

## What this forbids

- Half-screen empty teal wall with a small text cluster inside
- Fat full-width mid-chest bar
- White type sitting mid-coat with no plate
- 50/50 split that bisects his face when the graphic is only text or a short list
- Fat right newscast wall that eats the Napkin

## What this allows

- Compact fitted card, lower-left or lower-middle, face and hands clear
- List-plus-speaker only if the card is narrow and he sits in the clear half
- Real-diagram scenes: Napkin fills the frame; speaker in one of the four PIP seats
- Sequential diagram motion when he is explaining

## QC

Inspect real frames. Fail the stage if any box covers him, or if the PIP covers Napkin teaching. Fail the video if the last-scene disclaimer is missing or rewritten.
