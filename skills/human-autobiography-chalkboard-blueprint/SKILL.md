# Human Autobiography — Higgsfield production blueprint

Give Higgsfield this blueprint first. Then generate five sequences separately. Do not invent creative direction. Do not generate the whole film as one clip.

## Format
- 16:9 landscape
- Premium chalkboard animation
- Calm, continuously evolving
- Mostly static frontal board, occasional subtle push-in/pan
- No logos, no watermarks

## Brand
- Background: Deep Teal `#12343B` with subtle chalkboard texture
- Primary chalk: white (text, diagrams, figures, arrows, outlines)
- Accent: Signal Yellow `#F5D10A` only for key terms, circles, arrows, underlines, important questions
- White stays dominant. Never flood yellow.
- Hand-drawn chalk, dust, imperfect edges, live draw/erase
- Avoid glossy 3D, photoreal, cartoons, corporate icons, neon, gradients, long paragraphs

## Sequences
- **A** Scenes 1–4: Human Autobiography → Vasanas → Gunas → personality spectrum
- **B** Scenes 5–9: PFT / BMI / OET reveal → BMI → PFT → OET → central equation
- **C** Scenes 10–12: Search for happiness → three levels → permanence vs change
- **D** Scenes 13–15: Tamas→Rajas→Sattva → mastering BMI → changing question
- **E** Scenes 16–22: Synthesis → Who am I becoming?

Every sequence prompt must repeat the visual constraints (teal board, white chalk, yellow accent, no logos, live chalk-draw, same handwriting).

## Spell exactly
VASANAS, TAMAS, RAJAS, SATTVA, BODY, MIND, INTELLECT, PERCEIVER, FEELER, THINKER, OBJECTS, EMOTIONS, THOUGHTS, BMI, PFT, OET, PERMANENT HAPPINESS, WHO AM I BECOMING?

## Assembly
Generate sequences separately, then assemble sequentially. One chalkboard lesson, not disconnected slides.
