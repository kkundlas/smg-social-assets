# List reel

Use when Kulmeet says **list reel**, **/list-reel**, or wants a one-take talking-head Reel whose left-side numbered list fills in as he talks. Apply [Shield Medical brand](sand-workflow:shield-medical-brand). This is not the 7-still [facebook-reel](sand-workflow:facebook-reel), not the diagram [facebook-explainer](sand-workflow:facebook-explainer), and not [kk3-news](sand-workflow:kk3-news).

Technique lock (copy the edit, not the person, topic, or claims): Dallin Holt Facebook Reel `https://www.facebook.com/reel/4510269485912634` (45.6s, 1080×1920). Local ref: `/workspace/fb-style-ref/source.mp4` and `/workspace/fb-style-ref/frames/`.

## Locked shape

- Canvas **1080×1920**, about **45 seconds**.
- **One unbroken take.** Medium close-up, chest-up, eye level, looking into the lens. Indoor natural light, shallow depth of field.
- Zero cuts. Zero zooms. Zero punch-ins. Zero B-roll. Zero logo sting. Zero intro card.

## Locked graphics (on the whole time)

1. **Top center title.** White rounded pill, bold black sans-serif, one-line hook. Directly under it a smaller black rounded pill, white text: `(in N steps)`. Never leaves the frame. No “intro,” “hook,” or slide numbers.
2. **Left-side list.** Numbers `1.` through `N.` visible from frame 1, white with a thin black shadow. When that step is spoken, the line first types the **equation**, then snaps to the **result**. Do not dump the full list at the start.
3. **Bottom karaoke captions.** Bold sans-serif, 2–5 words, white with black outline, no caption box. The word being spoken right now is bright yellow (`#F5D10A` for Shield).
4. **Last 2 seconds.** Swap the caption for `Save for later` (white) with `later` in yellow.

## Locked audio

On-camera voice only. No music. No SFX on the text pops.

## Pacing

Jump straight into `Number one…` No welcome. One step every ~8–10 seconds. N defaults to 5 unless the topic needs 3–7.

## Shield version

- Patient-facing: Dr. Kundlas. Honest. No miracle claims. Educational only in the caption, not on the art unless he asks.
- On-camera is Kulmeet’s original footage only. Never clone face or voice.
- Topic shape: `How [everyday health thing] actually works (in N steps)` with real sourced numbers, never invented.
- Brand: Signal Yellow for the live caption word, Deep Teal / cream / Soft Black as needed. Flat Shield logo is **not** on this format unless Kulmeet asks (the reference has none).
- Caption (Buffer): one-line hook, the N steps in prose, phone `(863) 236-9550`, `— Dr. Kundlas, Shield Medical Group`, `Educational only — please talk to your clinician about you.`, location hashtags. Exactly three verified partner tags per page when posting.

## Workflow

1. Topic + hook line + N. Wait.
2. Write the spoken script and the list (equation → result per line). Wait.
3. Shoot or use original A/V. Overlay the locked graphics. Show the cut. Wait. Do not post.
4. After yes: host the MP4 on `kkundlas/smg-social-assets`, then [shield-facebook-publish](sand-workflow:shield-facebook-publish) as `fb_type: reel`, `customScheduled`. Never `shareNow` unless Kulmeet says post now.

## Never

Do not render before script approval. Do not publish before cut approval. Do not add music. Do not cut the take. Do not copy the reference creator’s face, finance math, or claims. Do not mix Kundlas MD YouTube branding onto Shield Facebook.
