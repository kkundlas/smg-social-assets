# Efficient production without quality loss

Reduce rework, not inspection. Treat these as planning targets rather than deadlines.

## Active-time target

For a typical 12–15-minute KundlasMD program, target 90–150 minutes of active editorial work. Track render, synchronization, transcription, and upload waiting separately. Never skip a gate to meet the target.

Suggested active-time allocation:

- source, transcript, retakes, pauses: 20–30 minutes;
- hook, chapters, micro-scenes, and card rail: 15–20 minutes;
- batched asset production: 30–45 minutes;
- asset preflight: 10–15 minutes;
- timeline assembly: 25–40 minutes;
- full-program QC and revision reconciliation: 15–30 minutes.

## Batch order

1. Resolve all retakes, false starts, pauses, and transcript corrections before graphics timing.
2. Approve the complete chapter and micro-scene rail once.
3. Assign every asset, presenter mode, semantic overlay, and tool before generation.
4. Generate assets chapter-by-chapter, grouping requests by Napkin AI, Remotion, Hyperframes, Canva, Higgsfield, icons, and B-roll when the tools permit efficient batches.
5. Inspect all outputs before timeline assembly. Repair class-wide failures in the source preset, then regenerate the affected batch.
6. Assemble only frozen assets.
7. Export one mobile review copy and collect feedback into a single revision log before revising.
8. Start the 4K render and Descript upload only after approval; treat their elapsed time as unattended machine time.

## Reusable scene system

Use these standard compositions before inventing a new layout:

- first-30-second hook;
- chapter card;
- presenter full frame;
- presenter-left with graphic-right;
- presenter-right with graphic-left;
- full-screen dense diagram;
- low-middle semantic sentence;
- evidence or checklist card;
- medical disclaimer;
- CTA and end screen.

Each preset must already enforce one compositor-owned logo, recorded-background preservation, safe headroom, mouth clearance, bottom-control clearance, KundlasMD fonts and colors, and 4K-safe dimensions.

## Anti-rework rules

- Do not create assets before the card rail is frozen.
- Do not place uninspected assets on the timeline.
- Do not repair duplicate logos, caption placement, or presenter framing scene-by-scene; repair the shared preset and rerun every affected scene.
- Do not render multiple review copies for comments received separately; consolidate feedback first.
- Do not create custom motion when an approved preset communicates the idea equally well.
- Do not count waiting for rendering, synchronization, or upload as active editing time.

## Quality floor

Never accelerate by skipping complete-thought dialogue review, medical-meaning review, asset inspection, full-program visual QC, SRT validation, 4K probing and decode, or verified Descript import.
