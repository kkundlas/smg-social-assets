# Whiteboard video

~20 second 9:16 slideshow. Whiteboard / marker-sketch stills, hard cuts, karaoke captions, voiceover. Looks like a marker board, not a talking head and not a drawn animation.

Not [Sketch reel](sand-workflow:sketch-reel) (that is 2–5 branded stills with motion on the drawing, no CapCut pile). Not Daily reel. Not chalkboard.

## How he invokes it

He says **create Whiteboard video** plus one sentence, for example: `create Whiteboard video: why patience is the wrong advice on a GLP-1`. If he names no topic, ask for one sentence.

## Locked recipe (from the 20s whiteboard Reels template)

1. Write 8–9 adult PCP / patient-education panels. One idea per panel. Educational only. Original gags and labels. Do not copy another page’s drawings, lines, or Elon/hustle script.
2. Still images only: black marker on a white board (optional yellow/red emphasis on one word in the drawing). Stick figures, graphs, simple metaphors. Each panel is a full 1080×1920 frame. Subtle Ken Burns (slow push) is allowed. No character animation, no rope-snap, no image-to-video of people moving.
3. Hard jump cuts about every 2–2.5 seconds. One fast blur/morph is optional. Runtime 18–22s. 30 fps.
4. Burned-in karaoke captions, lower-center: white words, yellow on the stressed word, thick black outline, one keyword at a time, synced to the voice. Labels that belong to the joke stay inside the drawing, not in the caption pile.
5. Audio: his original recorded voiceover, plus a licensed dramatic/ambient bed (Rhythmic Reverie or equivalent already on file). Never clone his voice or face. Never rip Facebook/TikTok “original audio.” If he has not recorded a take, build captions-only (or show stills) and wait for the take. Do not invent lines in his voice.
6. Branding: small Shield Medical Group logo for Facebook, Kundlas MD for TikTok, not covering captions or labels. Whiteboard field stays white (this product is the board, not the teal news card).
7. Show stills + MP4 + caption in chat. Wait. Never post until an explicit yes. Then GitHub `kkundlas/smg-social-assets` and hand Facebook / TikTok as he names. Never native-post.

## Never

- Do not clone a viral whiteboard page or its script.
- Do not clone his face or voice.
- Do not print PEACE, Miller, or chapter numbers.
- Do not use this slot for Daily reel or Sketch reel.
