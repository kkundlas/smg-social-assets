# Kundlas MD Make Long Video

Invoke: he says **make long video** and names (or pastes) the Descript project. Do not ask him to export files. After the project is named, do not ask permission between steps. Pause only for medical ambiguity, a thought-dropping cut, missing source, new paid/privacy risk, a plan change, or an auth wall.

Also follow [Kundlas MD Video Production](sand-workflow:kundlas-md-video-production), [Kundlas MD Speaker Clear](sand-workflow:kundlas-md-speaker-clear), [Kundlas MD Napkin Speaker Seats](sand-workflow:kundlas-md-napkin-speaker-seats), and [SMG YouTube Layout Pack](sand-workflow:smg-youtube-layout-pack).

Original A/V only. No voice or face clone. One KundlasMD logo PNG, small upper-left, no backing box. No Shield on screen. No music unless he asks.

## House rules (every step)

These were locked on Prediabetes Diet Mistakes (2026-08-24). Skipping any of them fails the video.

- Boxes cannot cover him. Size and place the box. Never a half-screen empty teal wall. Never a fat mid-chest bar. Never white type mid-coat with no plate.
- Banner anatomy: dark teal, yellow kicker, white claim, red underline (MYTH 5 / MISTAKE 2). Shape can change. Anatomy cannot.
- Red underline sits **under** the word ink, never through the letters. Every caption.
- Text-only or a short list: he stays full frame plus a fitted card (lower-left or lower-middle, face and hands clear).
- Real diagram (Napkin / Hyperframes / Remotion): that picture is the whole background. He is a small PIP. Four seats, pick by format and empty space: (1) portrait, window high; (2) portrait, small video bottom-left; (3) landscape rectangle bottom-left; (4) landscape circle bottom-left. YouTube long-form uses 3 or 4. Shorts use 1 or 2.
- Nothing overlaps. If the PIP hits type or icons, move the window or reflow the graphic. Crop-to-fill the PIP. Never stretch. Never a fat right newscast wall.
- Hold graphics through the spoken idea. Captions 2.5–4s or the phrase. Chapter/text/speaker 5s min. List/paragraph/quote/bigfact and Hyperframes 6–8s min. Do not flash.
- Teaching chapters get real motion when he is explaining a mechanism (PAIRING CHANGES THE CURVE, THE PLATE METHOD). More motion, not stuffing. No Higgsfield or Canva quota per chapter.
- Subscribe 3–4 times about every 3 minutes, plus the end screen, then the locked DISCLAIMER card (exact three sentences, full teal, 8s+).
- YouTube audio: −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP, AAC 48 kHz. Descript −16 is podcast only.
- Intelligent captions: SMG keys only. 6–8 words, one yellow word. Not SRT-on-picture.
- Generated icons/b-roll first (built-in image model). Canva is for planned B-roll links and backup. Never generate his face. Yellow never on light.

## Step 1 — Clean the talk

Pull the named Descript project into that video’s Borumi folder (`/Borumi/YYYY-MM-DD - <title>`). Inspect source. Clean transcript: retakes, filler, gaps, hook. Keep original A/V. Do not make chapters or graphics yet. Do not encode 4K yet.

## Step 2 — Write the plan first (show him)

One plan, chapter by chapter, **before any asset file is made**. Chapter count comes from the talk, not a keyword quota. Name chapters with the primary and secondary long-tail keywords when those names still match what he said. Do not invent a chapter to chase a phrase.

Each chapter row must log:

- chapter title
- every intelligent caption: SMG key, exact words, yellow word, seat (lower-left / lower-middle / PIP-safe)
- every picture: Napkin, Hyperframes, Remotion, generated still, Higgsfield, Canva B-roll
- for Canva: search, pick, **store the Canva links in the row**. Do not download or wire until assemble
- empty cell means none

Show him this plan. Nothing gets built until the row exists. No improvising new cards on the timeline later.

## Step 3 — Make only what the plan listed

Produce those files. Inspect frames. Freeze them. Do not add extras.

## Step 4 — Assemble from the plan

Full-frame him on text cards. Napkin/diagram as canvas + one of the four PIP seats. Subscribe cadence. Exact DISCLAIMER last. −14 LUFS. Proof frames of flagged beats.

## Step 5 — He watches

Deliver a slim phone review (and 1080 if useful) to Desktop and chat. Collect notes. **Amend the plan first**, then make only the new pieces, then rebuild. The written plan locks the first assemble, not the last.

## Step 6 — 4K, YouTube, Descript, send links

When he says the review is good (or asks for 4K):

1. Encode the locked cut to 3840×2160 H.264, AAC 48 kHz. Copy already-loudnormed audio if it is already −14 / −1.
2. Probe. Do not call it 4K unless width=3840 and height=2160.
3. Upload that exact file to Kundlas MD YouTube as **unlisted** unless he named another visibility. Title from the video title. Not made for kids. Monetization off unless he asks.
4. Send the watch URL in chat immediately (`youtu.be` or `youtube.com/watch?v=`).
5. Import the same file into **that job’s existing Descript project** as `04 Deliver/<title>-4K.mp4`. Direct upload, wait until import `success`. Do not drop it on the talking-head composition. Do not `publish_project` unless he asks.
6. Send the Descript project URL.

## Fail the video if

Last-scene disclaimer missing or rewritten. A box covers him. A PIP covers Napkin teaching. Audio left at −16. 4K claimed without 3840×2160. YouTube or Descript skipped after a 4K exists. Assets made that were not on the plan.
