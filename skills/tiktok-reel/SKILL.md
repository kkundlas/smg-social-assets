# TikTok reel

Produce 1 MP4 (1080×1920), about 21–45s. Independent of Facebook. Do not clone a Facebook reel’s logo-first chrome. Apply [Shield Medical brand](sand-workflow:shield-medical-brand) as light accents only. Default brand is Shield. Use Kundlas MD only if Kulmeet says this video is Kundlas MD. Never auto-publish.

## What is different from Facebook

- One account: TikTok `@kundlasmd`. Not two local Pages.
- Hook in the first second. No logo sting. No “welcome.” No BOOK YOUR VISIT in frame 1.
- Brand is light: Signal Yellow captions, Deep Teal accents, flat Shield (or Kundlas MD) mark on the last 1–2 seconds only.
- Soft CTA: comment, save, follow. Phone and book-now belong in the caption, not burned in up front.
- No partner Page tags. 3–5 topical hashtags max.

## Locked look

- Canvas 1080×1920. Safe zone: keep essential type out of the right rail and bottom caption area.
- Hook is spoken or huge on-screen type in second 1. One idea per beat.
- Fonts: Bebas Neue for short all-caps (≤6 words). Lato for everything else.
- Colors: Deep Teal `#12343B`, Signal Yellow `#F5D10A`, Warm Off-White `#F4F3EE`, Soft Black `#231F20`, Crimson `#96151D` as a small accent only. Never `#F72736`. Never yellow on cream.
- Burned-in captions: 2–5 words, white with black outline, live word in Signal Yellow.
- Original A/V only. Never clone Kulmeet’s face or voice.

## Locked 7-beat shape (adapt copy, do not print slide numbers)

1. Hook question or scroll-stopper (no “BREAKING HEALTH NEWS”).
2. The local or everyday fact.
3. Why it hits the viewer.
4. One sourced evidence line (never invent a number).
5. The simple truth in two short sentences.
6. Three next steps, spoken or on-screen.
7. Soft CTA + tiny end card (logo, `@kundlasmd`). Not a flyer.

## Audio

On-camera or original voice preferred. If a bed is needed, Utope — Rhythmic Reverie at 0.29–0.36 under the voice. No meme SFX. No trending sound that fights the teaching.

## Copy rules

- Write the 7-beat on-screen + spoken text first. Wait for Kulmeet’s approval before rendering.
- Patient-facing: Dr. Kundlas. Contractions. Short sentences. Honest verbs (lower / manage / improve; never cure / reverse / prevent unless the evidence supports it).
- No PHI. No fear-mongering. No miracle language.
- Caption: hook, 3–5 hashtags, `— Dr. Kundlas`, `Educational only — please talk to your clinician about you.` Disclaimer lives in the caption, not on the frames (unless he asks).

## Workflow

1. Topic (or 2 options). Wait.
2. Write the 7-beat copy. Wait. Do not render yet.
3. Render the MP4 from original media and/or motion (Napkin / HyperFrames / Remotion as the visual lane). Inspect safe zone and hook.
4. Show the cut + caption. Wait. Do not post.
5. After yes: [Shield TikTok publish](sand-workflow:shield-tiktok-publish). Always `customScheduled`, never `shareNow`, unless he says post now.

## Never

Do not copy Facebook’s 480×150 logo hero, yellow-box flyer, or BOOK YOUR VISIT frame. Do not mix Kundlas MD onto a Shield video unless he said so. Do not invent stats. Do not post without showing the cut and caption.
