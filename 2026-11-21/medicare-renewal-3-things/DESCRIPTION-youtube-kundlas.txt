If you do nothing during Medicare Open Enrollment, most plans still renew on their own. Before December 7, check three things: are your doctors and hospitals still in network for 2027, are your prescriptions still on next year's drug list, and what does your Annual Notice of Change say about premiums, copays, and benefits. You can compare plans on the Medicare Plan Finder at Medicare.gov.
