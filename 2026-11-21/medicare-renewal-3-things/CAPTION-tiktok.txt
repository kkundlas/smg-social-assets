Medicare Open Enrollment ends December 7. If you don't do anything, most Medicare plans renew on their own.

Before that date, check three things. Are your doctors and hospitals still in network for 2027? Are your prescriptions still on next year's drug list? And what does your Annual Notice of Change say about next year's premium, copays, and benefits?

You can compare plans on the Medicare Plan Finder at Medicare.gov. Save this so you remember before December 7.
