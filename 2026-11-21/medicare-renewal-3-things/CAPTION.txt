Medicare Open Enrollment ends December 7. If you don't do anything, most Medicare plans still renew on their own.

Before that date, check three things: whether your doctors and hospitals are still in network for 2027, whether your prescriptions stay on next year's drug list, and what your Annual Notice of Change says about next year's premium, copays, and benefit changes. Compare plans on the Medicare Plan Finder at Medicare.gov.

Save this so you can check before December 7.
