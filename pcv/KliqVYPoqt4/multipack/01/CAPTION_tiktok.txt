You've seen headlines saying Medicare is dropping you, or that millions of seniors are about to lose their coverage overnight. Don't panic, but don't ignore that letter either.

Headlines can make it sound like Medicare is dropping you. Private plans may not renew, but the federal Medicare program itself is not canceling coverage.
