Headlines can make it sound like Medicare is dropping you overnight.

Private insurance companies are choosing not to renew some plans, and a few are leaving certain counties. That part is real.
The federal Medicare program itself is not canceling coverage. CMS, the agency that runs Medicare, says the 2027 Medicare Advantage and Part D market is expected to remain stable.
Nationally, the total number of plans is only dropping from 5,553 to about 5,532. That's roughly 21 fewer plans, net, across the entire country.

Try this:
If you got a letter, read it carefully
Don't assume Medicare itself is ending
Don't ignore the letter either
Ask a counselor if the wording is unclear

Don't panic, and don't throw the letter away.
