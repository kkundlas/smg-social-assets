Your Medicare Plan May End. Medicare Itself Is Not Canceling
Source: https://youtu.be/KliqVYPoqt4 (00:00-01:44)

[IMPORTANT UPDATE]
You've seen headlines saying Medicare is dropping you, or that millions of seniors are about to lose their coverage overnight. Don't panic, but don't ignore that letter either.

[WHAT THIS MEANS]
Private insurance companies are choosing not to renew some plans, and a few are leaving certain counties. That part is real.

[WHY THIS MATTERS]
But the federal Medicare program itself isn't canceling anything. There's real change for some people, and also a lot of exaggeration online.

[WHAT THE EVIDENCE SHOWS]
CMS, the agency that runs Medicare, says the 2027 Medicare Advantage and Part D market is expected to remain stable.

[WHAT YOU SHOULD DO NEXT]
Nationally, the total number of plans is only dropping from 5,553 to about 5,532. That's roughly 21 fewer plans, net, across the entire country.

[FINAL TAKEAWAY]
So if you got a letter, read it carefully, and don't assume Medicare itself is ending. Watch the full video, link in the description.
