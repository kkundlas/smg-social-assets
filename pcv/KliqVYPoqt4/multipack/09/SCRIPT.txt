A Checklist Before December 7
Source: https://youtu.be/KliqVYPoqt4 (08:00-09:15)

[IMPORTANT UPDATE]
Here's a practical checklist before December 7.

[WHAT THIS MEANS]
First, check your mail for an Annual Notice of Change, and a Plan Non-Renewal Notice. Medicare.gov says that notice is mailed in October if your plan is leaving.

[WHY THIS MATTERS]
This matters even if your plan isn't going anywhere. Copays, drug lists, and doctor networks can change in January on the same plan.

[WHAT THE EVIDENCE SHOWS]
Second, don't trust a directory or a TV ad number alone. Call your plan and your doctor's billing office to confirm your doctors and medications for 2027. Write down who you spoke with, and the date and time.

[WHAT YOU SHOULD DO NEXT]
Third, get free, unbiased help from SHIP, the State Health Insurance Assistance Program. Federally funded, about 2,000 local offices help up to 4 million people a year, and none work on commission.

[FINAL TAKEAWAY]
Find a counselor at shiphelp.org, or call 1-800-MEDICARE, that's 1-800-633-4227. Watch the full video, link in the description.
