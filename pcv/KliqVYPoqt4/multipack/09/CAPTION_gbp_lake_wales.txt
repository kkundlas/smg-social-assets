Here's a practical checklist to use before December 7.

Check your mail for an Annual Notice of Change, and a Plan Non-Renewal Notice. Medicare.gov says that notice is mailed in October if your plan is leaving.
Even if your plan isn't going anywhere, copays, drug lists, and doctor networks can change in January on the same plan.
Don't trust a directory or a TV ad alone. Confirm your doctors and medications for 2027 with your plan and your doctor's billing office, and write down who you spoke with.
