I want to slow down on that guaranteed-issue window, because it is a federal right, not a loophole.

If your Medicare Advantage plan is leaving and you switch to Original Medicare, you may have a federal right to buy certain Medigap policies. That right does not generally apply if you choose another Medicare Advantage plan instead.
