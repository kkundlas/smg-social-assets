The Medigap Guaranteed-Issue Window Has a Tradeoff
Source: https://youtu.be/KliqVYPoqt4 (04:43-05:43)

[IMPORTANT UPDATE]
I want to slow down on that guaranteed-issue window, because it is a federal right, not a loophole.

[WHAT THIS MEANS]
If your Medicare Advantage plan leaves Medicare or stops serving your area, and you switch to Original Medicare, you can buy certain Medigap policies in that 123-day window.

[WHY THIS MATTERS]
That means no health questions, no denials, and no higher price for a pre-existing condition.

[WHAT THE EVIDENCE SHOWS]
Here's the tradeoff worth understanding. That particular right is tied to going back to Original Medicare.

[WHAT YOU SHOULD DO NEXT]
If you choose a different Medicare Advantage plan instead, you generally do not get this particular Medigap guaranteed-issue right.
