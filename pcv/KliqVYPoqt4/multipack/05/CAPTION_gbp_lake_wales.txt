If your Medicare Advantage plan is leaving, you may have a federal right to buy Medigap. It is a right, not a loophole.

If your plan leaves Medicare or stops serving your area, and you switch to Original Medicare, you can buy certain Medigap policies in that 123-day window.
That means no health questions, no denials, and no higher price for a pre-existing condition.
That particular right is tied to going back to Original Medicare. If you choose a different Medicare Advantage plan instead, you generally do not get this right.
