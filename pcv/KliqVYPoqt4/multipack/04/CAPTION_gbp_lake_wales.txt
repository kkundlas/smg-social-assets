Do you have to switch before January 1st? That deadline is too strict.

Open Enrollment for 2027 coverage runs from October 15 to December 7, 2026. That's the window most people should use.
If your plan isn't being renewed, a Special Enrollment Period can run through the last day of February.
If you're using your federal right to buy Medigap because your Medicare Advantage plan is leaving, that window runs 60 days before coverage ends through 63 days after, 123 days total, often into early March.

Try this:
Use Open Enrollment if you can, through December 7, 2026
If your plan is not being renewed, you may have more time
A Medigap guaranteed-issue window can last 123 days, with no rejection or higher price for a pre-existing condition
