Prior Authorization Is Far More Common in Medicare Advantage
Source: https://youtu.be/KliqVYPoqt4 (05:43-07:13)

[IMPORTANT UPDATE]
I'll give you the prior authorization numbers, no spin.

[WHAT THIS MEANS]
Traditional Medicare had just over 625,000 prior authorization requests in fiscal year 2024. Medicare Advantage had 52.8 million determinations, about 85 times as many.

[WHY THIS MATTERS]
That's not a perfectly even comparison, because traditional Medicare requires prior authorization for far fewer services. But the difference in volume is real.

[WHAT THE EVIDENCE SHOWS]
A KFF study from January 2026 found 4.1 million full or partial denials out of those 52.8 million determinations, about 7.7 percent.

[WHAT YOU SHOULD DO NEXT]
This shows up most in post-acute care, like inpatient rehab or long-term care hospital stays.

[FINAL TAKEAWAY]
KFF, summarizing HHS Office of Inspector General data from June 2024 for large Medicare Advantage organizations, found a 54 percent initial denial rate for inpatient rehabilitation requests, and 65 percent for long-term care hospital requests, compared with under 8 percent overall.
