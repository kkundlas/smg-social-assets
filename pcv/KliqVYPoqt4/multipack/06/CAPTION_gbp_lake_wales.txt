Here are the prior authorization numbers, with no spin.

Traditional Medicare had just over 625,000 prior authorization requests in fiscal year 2024. Medicare Advantage had 52.8 million determinations, about 85 times as many.
That's not a perfectly even comparison, since traditional Medicare requires prior authorization for far fewer services. The volume gap is still real.
A KFF study from January 2026 found 4.1 million full or partial denials, about 7.7 percent, mostly in post-acute care.
