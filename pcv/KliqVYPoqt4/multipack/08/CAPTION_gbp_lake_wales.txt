On average, Medicare Advantage enrollees had access to about half the physicians available on traditional Medicare in the same area.

KFF found that in 2022, that figure was 48 percent. So the network you assume is wide may be narrower than traditional Medicare in the same place.
That's one reason it's worth double-checking that your actual doctors are in-network for next year, not just trusting the directory.
A plan can look fine on paper and still leave a doctor out of network. This matters if you want to keep the same doctors in 2027.

Try this:
Confirm the doctors you already see, not just a name on a list
Don't rely on the directory alone
Check the network before you stay or switch
