Medicare Advantage Networks Cover About Half the Doctors
Source: https://youtu.be/KliqVYPoqt4 (07:33-08:00)

[IMPORTANT UPDATE]
KFF found that in 2022, Medicare Advantage enrollees had access, on average, to 48 percent of the physicians available to someone on traditional Medicare in the same area.

[WHAT THIS MEANS]
That's about half. So the network you assume is wide may be narrower than traditional Medicare in the same place.

[WHY THIS MATTERS]
That's one reason it's worth double-checking that your actual doctors are in-network for next year, not just trusting the directory.

[WHAT THE EVIDENCE SHOWS]
Don't just trust the directory. Confirm the doctors you already see, not just a name on a list.

[WHAT YOU SHOULD DO NEXT]
This matters if you want to keep the same doctors in 2027. A plan can look fine on paper and still leave a doctor out of network.

[FINAL TAKEAWAY]
So before you stay or switch, confirm your doctors are in-network for next year. Watch the full video, link in the description.
