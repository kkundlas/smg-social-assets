The 5 Million Losing Coverage Number Isn't Accurate
Source: https://youtu.be/KliqVYPoqt4 (01:44-02:18)

[IMPORTANT UPDATE]
So how many people does this actually affect? You'll see figures online as high as 5 million. That high-end number isn't accurate.

[WHAT THIS MEANS]
A common published range is about 2.4 million people in plans that are ending or not being renewed.

[WHY THIS MATTERS]
Other analyses of CMS files put it around 2.8 to 3.15 million who must pick a new plan.

[WHAT THE EVIDENCE SHOWS]
And CMS says about 8 in 10 Medicare Advantage enrollees can stay in their current plan, at the same premium or a lower one.

[WHAT YOU SHOULD DO NEXT]
So if your plan is ending, you will need to choose a new one. But most people can stay in the plan they have now.

[FINAL TAKEAWAY]
Don't rely on the biggest number you see online. Check what is actually happening with your own plan. Watch the full video, link in the description.
