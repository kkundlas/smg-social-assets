You'll see figures online as high as 5 million people losing coverage. That high-end number isn't accurate.

A common published range is about 2.4 million people in plans that are ending or not being renewed.
Other analyses of CMS files put it around 2.8 to 3.15 million who must pick a new plan.
CMS says about 8 in 10 Medicare Advantage enrollees can stay in their current plan, at the same premium or a lower one.

Try this:
Don't rely on the biggest number you see online
Check what is happening with your own plan
If your plan is ending, you will need to choose a new one
Most people can stay in the plan they have now
