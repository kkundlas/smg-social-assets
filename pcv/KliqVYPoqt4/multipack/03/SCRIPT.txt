You're Not Locked Out of Original Medicare
Source: https://youtu.be/KliqVYPoqt4 (02:18-03:48)

[IMPORTANT UPDATE]
You're not locked out of Original Medicare. That's just not true. Original Medicare is always available, and it never goes away.

[WHAT THIS MEANS]
You can also choose a different Medicare Advantage plan during Open Enrollment. Medigap, or Medicare Supplement, is the part that gets harder.

[WHY THIS MATTERS]
When you first turn 65 and enroll in Part B, you get a one-time, six-month window to buy a Medigap policy with no health questions.

[WHAT THE EVIDENCE SHOWS]
Once that window closes, in most states, a Medigap insurer can ask about your health history, and can deny you or charge you more.

[WHAT YOU SHOULD DO NEXT]
KFF found in 2023 that 74 percent of full-year Medicare Advantage enrollees, about 20.9 million people, had a health condition that could lead to a Medigap denial outside a guaranteed-issue period.

[FINAL TAKEAWAY]
Could lead to a denial, not that a denial is guaranteed. This is about Medigap, not Medicare itself. Watch the full video, link in the description.
