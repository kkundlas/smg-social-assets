You're not locked out of Original Medicare. That's just not true.

Original Medicare is always available, and it never goes away. You can also choose a different Medicare Advantage plan during Open Enrollment.
Medigap, or Medicare Supplement, is the part that gets harder. When you first turn 65 and enroll in Part B, you get a one-time, six-month window to buy a Medigap policy with no health questions.
Once that window closes, in most states, a Medigap insurer can ask about your health history, and can deny you or charge you more.

Try this:
Original Medicare itself remains available
Medigap rules are a separate question
A health condition could lead to a Medigap denial outside a guaranteed-issue period
Could lead to a denial, not that a denial is guaranteed
