Only 11.5 percent of 4.1 million Medicare Advantage denials were ever appealed.

That means most people simply didn't challenge the decision. They stopped at the first no.
When people did appeal, 80.7 percent of those appeals were overturned. That is most of the appeals that were filed.
That doesn't prove every single denial was wrong. Keep that in mind when you look at the 80.7 percent figure. It still tells you appealing is worth your time, and most people simply don't do it.

Try this:
If your plan denies care, don't stop at the first no
Most denials are never appealed
Most of the appeals that were filed were overturned
The high overturn rate does not prove every denial was wrong

If a needed service is denied, ask how to appeal rather than stopping at the first no.
